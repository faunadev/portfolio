My Journey

A timeline of how I started, learned, built, and grew as both a developer and a graphic designer.

  1. 2022Where it started

    Started learning the basics of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript while beginning my first year as a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology student. This was when I first started getting comfortable with coding and building simple things on my own.

  2. 2023Exploring & creating

    Started experimenting with graphic design and Canva while continuing to improve my coding skills. I also began building simple landing pages and trying different ways to bring my ideas to life.

  3. 2024Building real projects

    Built my first fully working system as a school project, with the help of AI along the way, then went on to create more systems and projects. I also started working as a barista at a local coffee shop, and kept doing graphic design from time to time as a hobby and another way to be creative.

  4. 2025Starting freelance work

    Started working as a local freelancer in web development and graphic design, occasionally using AI to help work through problems along the way. Most of my first clients came through friends and friends of friends, giving me my first experience working on projects for other people.

  5. 2026Apps, internship & graduation

    Expanded into mobile app development and built more advanced projects. I also completed my internship, gained hands-on experience, and graduated with a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology.
